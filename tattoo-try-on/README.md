# Tattoo Try-On

A single-file, in-browser tool for previewing tattoos on a photo of yourself. Nothing is uploaded; all processing happens on a canvas in the browser.

- Upload a photo, then add a design from the flash sheet, upload your own artwork (white paper is keyed out), or set lettering.
- Move, resize, rotate, mirror. **Curve** wraps the design around an arm or leg; **Bend** arcs it.
- Ink is multiplied into the skin so your skin tone and shading show through. Presets for Fresh, Healed and 10 years old adjust strength, softness and fading.
- **Erase** tucks the tattoo behind fingers or sleeves; **Restore** brings it back.
- Hold **Compare** (or `B`) to see the photo without the new tattoos; **Save image** renders the result.

`tattoo-try-on.html` is written as an Artifact page body (no `<html>`/`<head>` wrapper) but also opens directly in a browser.
