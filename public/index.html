FAQ

Good questions.

Where does the data come from?

From Meta's public Ad Library, the same source anyone can browse by hand. Rivl collects it for you, keeps the history Meta doesn't show, and organises it into a report.

Do I need to connect my own ad account?

No. Rivl only looks at public ads, so there's nothing to connect and no access to your account.

Can I see how much a competitor spends?

Meta only publishes spend for political and social-issue ads. For other ads we show run length, number of variants and EU reach, which together tell you where the budget is going.

How fresh is the data?

Tracked competitors are refreshed every 24 hours. A brand-new search runs live and takes about 30 seconds.

Which countries are covered?

Every country where Meta runs ads. Filter by country to see what a competitor runs in one market versus another.
